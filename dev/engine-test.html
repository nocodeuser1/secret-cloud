Section one

The original idea

Collect around fifty audio loops of varying lengths, all in a similar vibe. You now have fifty MP3s, and that is the whole raw material for the first drop.

Physically go to a place. The running example is the park in Guthrie, because it is close, it is quiet after dinner, and it has a hill with a stone on top.

In the app's world model, place each loop at a spot, give it a radius of about thirty feet, a decibel level at the outer ring and a decibel level at the inner ring. Even with no map at all, you could follow your ears and explore the whole area as a listening experience.

Easter eggs: put a very tight radius on a special object, like a stone on top of a hill. People notice "I wonder what that stone is saying", walk up, and hear something only there.

Section two

Two sides of one thing

The first side is the walk-in sound experience you can wander into and choose your own adventure. Instruments show as symbols on the map, and the violin gets louder as you walk toward the rock where it was played.

The second side is secret live shows run from a band account. The app generates a QR code for the band, they post it around, and scanning it is the invite. Inside the radius during the show, you can enter an experience with the band.

The phone tension is real: it is getting taboo to hold your phone during an experience. The answer both of them landed on is that the phone should lead you there and then get out of the way.

Section three

Rabbit trails

These are the ideas that came up and were not decided. Vote on each one. The latest vote per person wins, and clicking your current vote again clears it.

A. Monthly drops

Once a space is designed, you can drop a new experience into the same park every month, so people keep coming back. A city might love it because it brings people to the park.

B. Replies and testimonies

Listeners can reply to whoever set up the soundscape. Because the app is not set up as religious, a reply is just a reply, and testimonies from people who felt something would come in naturally. See the two sides for how this fits.
