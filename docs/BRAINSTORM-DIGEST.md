# Brainstorm digest — the call that started The Secret Cloud

Condensed from a phone call in October 2026 between Israel and his bandmate (whose original idea this was). This digest is the single source of truth for the plan site. Everything in the site should be traceable to this document or clearly marked as an addition.

## Who is talking

- **Israel** — producer/engineer side. Runs an AI phone business, works with iPads for live rigs, thinks in systems. Will drive the build.
- **The bandmate** — had the original idea. Dreamer, writer, musician (his wife plays violin). Wants it to be "ours", not Israel's or his.
- The two of them, plus friends, jam and record **worship sessions and loops** together. They want to tell the rest of the group so everyone gets in on it.

The name they landed on: **The Secret Cloud**. It is the name of the group's secret club *and* the thing lives in the digital cloud. They liked that the parallels were "mind-blowing" and that they'd be "subverting the medium" by using phones to get people outside and into a real place.

## The original idea (the bandmate's)

1. Collect around **50 audio loops** of varying lengths, all in a similar vibe. You now have 50 MP3s.
2. Physically go to a place. The running example is **the park in Guthrie**.
3. In the app's world model, **place each loop at a spot**, give it a **radius** (say 30 feet), a **decibel level at the outer ring** and a **decibel level at the inner ring**.
4. Even with no map at all, you could **follow your ears** and explore the whole area as a listening experience. The space is curated.
5. **Easter eggs**: put a very tight radius on a special object, like a stone on top of a hill. People notice "I wonder what that stone is saying", walk up, and hear something only there. Tight-radius drops could be **Bible verses, prophetic words, life spoken over someone**. A ministry Israel was part of used to write hundreds of prophetic words a few times a year; the group could write its own.
6. **Monthly drops**: once a space is designed, you can drop a *new* experience into the same park every month, so people keep coming back. A city might love it because it brings people to the park.
7. The image that sells it: walking off the path into a little bit of forest because you heard something, and someone else walking in too.

## Israel's layers on top

- **Multi-track soundscapes.** Tie each instrument's gain to a location. There is a **perfect spot** to stand where you hear the mix the way it should sound on the album, in stereo. Walk toward the rock where the violin was played and the violin gets louder. Not true 3D audio, just louder as you approach. Instruments show as symbols on the map. Israel suggested this only for the soundscapes, so they stay special.
- **Use the live jams and worship sessions** as content too, not only produced loops.
- **Replies and testimonies.** Listeners can reply to whoever set up the soundscape. Because the app is not set up as "religious", a reply is just a reply, and testimonies from people who felt the presence of God would come in naturally. "It's all under the cover."
- **Band account.** Keep the walk-in soundscape idea and the live-show idea separate in the product, but interweave the functionality. A band account can **schedule live shows in an area**. Every show adds to the content and to a **regional archive**. A **heat map** shows travelers where most of this happens so they know where to look on a journey.
- **QR codes and secret shows.** The app generates a QR code for the band. They post it around (flyers, signs, online). Scanning it is the invite. When people show up, their phone gets an invitation to join the event locally; turn-by-turn directions to the radius. A "scavenger hunt for music", live performances only. Inside the radius during the show, you can enter an app experience with the band.
  - Ideas for that in-show experience: letting the crowd alter effects (probably a disaster with many people), or **visuals** on loop that match the vibe of that area, generated monthly (a few hours of AI video work per month).
  - **A reward for showing up** that nobody else gets, e.g. a recording of that live set.
  - **Follow** the band in the app and get notified each month.
- **The phone tension.** The bandmate: it's getting taboo to hold your phone during an experience; people are sick of it. Israel: they still *find* it through an app; the point is to get out and walk around, not stare at the screen. Both agree: the phone should lead you there and then get out of the way.
- **The mission filter.** "Find the right person, not the most people." Shows will be intimate and rare-repeat; depth over reach. If someone follows, they come back next month.
- **The West Virginia video.** A guy with a PA on a blanket in the woods playing loops from an iPad, and people slowly walking in from different parts of the forest. Israel's version: "we're actually there in worship, and people feel the presence of God because they followed a sign on the street." "God could tell us where to put those signs and the right people would come." The bandmate: "follow the white rabbit, only for the kingdom."
- **Gear.** Low-power is the constraint in the woods. An iPad can replace the amps (amp modeling) and run the whole rig. Israel loves the Microcosm granular pedal (~$400) but wants to do that on the iPad instead of adding cables. Granular effects on acoustic guitar and vocals.

## What they agreed

- Two sides of one thing: (1) the **walk-in sound experience** you can wander into and choose your own adventure, and (2) **secret live shows** run from a band account.
- It belongs to both of them. Tell the group. Get everyone excited.
- Israel will map out "what's possible and what's not" with AI. That is this site.
- The season they're in: hope overflowing, "the feast", dreaming again. The plan should feel like that.

## Vocabulary to keep

- **The Secret Cloud** — the club, the app, and the cloud it runs on.
- **Drop** — one piece of audio placed at a location with a radius and inner/outer levels.
- **Soundscape** — a set of drops that make one listening experience, with a perfect spot.
- **Easter egg** — a tight-radius drop on a specific object.
- **Perfect spot** — where the multi-track mix lines up.
- **Band account / secret show** — the live-performance side.
- **Monthly drop** — a new experience in the same place each month.
- **Reply / testimony** — what a listener sends back.

## Things that must stay true

- The public-facing app never preaches. The place does the talking. Curators choose what to say.
- It is about finding the right person, not reach. No feeds, no public counts, no likes.
- Phone leads you there, then gets out of the way.
- Same park, new experience every month, so a place becomes a habit.
